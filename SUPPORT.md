# Support

- **Bug in the template** (something broken or wrong): [open an issue](https://github.com/OpenADMET/blind-challenge-template/issues/new/choose).
- **Question, setup help, "how do I…"**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/OpenADMET/blind-challenge-template/discussions), or the OpenADMET community — see [openadmet.org](https://openadmet.org).
- **Security vulnerability**: do not open a public issue — follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This template is provided as-is and maintained on a best-effort basis. It is not a supported product, and the maintainers can't help debug a specific challenge deployment.
